## How to Use This Log

1. **Every meeting:** If a decision was made, add it here
2. **Reference:** Link back to meeting notes or Slack discussions
3. **Tracking:** Update status as implementation progresses
4. **Review:** Quarterly review of decisions and their outcomes
5. **Reverse:** If a decision is reversed, mark it and document why
